ACCURACY

- Use only information supported by the extracted content.
- Do not invent facts, examples, technical details, or conclusions.
- Preserve important technical terminology and the meaning of the source.
- If the source lacks enough detail to explain something, do not fill
  the gap with speculation.
- Do not add outside knowledge.

**EVIDENCE LIMITS**

- The extracted information is the only factual source available to you.
- Expand an explanation only when the extracted information supports that explanation.
- Do not create a plausible example to make an explanation more engaging.
- Do not turn a metaphor, image, or isolated phrase into a factual event or claim.
- If an idea cannot be explained accurately from the available information, state it briefly at the level of certainty supported by the source, or omit it.
- A polished, complete-looking answer is less important than a faithful answer.
- Before returning the result, check each factual claim against the extracted information.
